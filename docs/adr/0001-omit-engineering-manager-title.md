# Omit the Engineering Manager title from the site

The site presents Marco's current role as **Tech Lead · Imagonist (Zucchetti Group)** and deliberately does not show the prior **Engineering Manager** title in the role blocks. Leadership credibility is carried by tenure instead ("I've led this team since 2021").

## Why

On LinkedIn the EM → Tech Lead transition is disambiguated by two dated Experience entries, so it reads as a change of ownership, not a demotion. The site has no equivalent dated chronology: an inline "was Engineering Manager, now Tech Lead" would be exposed to a demotion-reading. We judged the tenure line ("led this team since 2021") sufficient to carry the seniority signal without that risk.

## Note for future editors

Do not "fix" the omission by re-adding the Engineering Manager title to the role block — it is intentional. The EM history lives on LinkedIn, where the dated structure makes it safe.

Related: **iOS** is kept in the meta keywords, JSON-LD `knowsAbout`, and the header for search and identity, even though the current mobile stack is Flutter. That is also intentional (background/keyword, not current stack) and should not be removed as "outdated."
